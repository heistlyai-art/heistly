File 03 — Switch

Change the bills without stepping outside.

A switch trades one sealed asset for another inside the vault. ETH, USDC, and USDG. You do not withdraw to a public wallet, swap in the open, and deposit again. That round trip is the trail a switch is meant to skip.

Inside the vault

Three ways across the same desk.

ETH → USDC

Into dollars

A sealed ether note becomes a sealed dollar note. The trade does not surface as a swap from your address.

USDC → USDG

Bill for bill

Move between the two dollar assets without parking either one on a public exchange in your name.

USDG → ETH

Back to ether

When the next payment wants ETH, the switch settles it as a new note. Then a getaway can spend that note.

How it settles

Notes in. Notes out.

You prove you hold the note you are spending. The vault settles the other asset as a new note. Your wallet does not sign a public swap, because your wallet is not in the trade.

What does not happen

No hop out to a public pool with your address on both sides. No second deposit to “come back inside.” If a switch ever required that, it would not be this desk.

The quote, the route, and the price are part of the app that is not open yet. This page does not invent a rate.
