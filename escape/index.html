File 04 — Escape

Cross the chain. Do not announce the walk.

An escape route is a private bridge. A note leaves on one chain and is delivered on another. Your wallet does not hop through a public bridge contract under its own name. The arrival is a delivery, the same shape as a getaway.

The routes on the brief

Five chains. Home is Robinhood.

These are the lanes the desk is drawn for. None of them is live on this site. $HEIST stays on Robinhood Chain even when a note escapes elsewhere.

The chain files

Shape of a crossing

It is still a getaway.

You seal or already hold a note. You prove it locally. A Driver posts on the destination chain and pays the gas there. The address you named receives the asset. Your wallet is not the sender on that transaction.

What this page will not pretend

There is no live route, no fee schedule, and no promised time. When an escape opens, the chain page and this file get the specifics. Until then the map is the brief, not a button that moves money.
