File 08 — Docs

The short brief.

Enough to know what the words mean, and what this website is not. It is not a live app, not an API reference, and not a place where a contract address is implied.

Glossary

The words on the desk.

Note
The encrypted record of a seal: asset, amount, and a secret. You hold it. Heistly is not built to keep a copy.
Vault
Where ETH, USDC, and USDG are sealed. The chain sees the deposit. The note is what makes a later private send possible.
Proof
A zero-knowledge proof, built on your side, that you hold an unspent note. It does not name which note.
Driver
The relayer. It pays gas and posts the getaway. It does not take a seed and it does not choose the recipient.
Getaway
A private send. The destination is paid from the vault. Your wallet does not sign that transaction.
Switch
A trade among ETH, USDC, and USDG that settles as notes, inside the vault.
Escape
A private bridge across Robinhood Chain, Base, Arbitrum, BNB Chain, or Solana.
Safehouse
Recovery from a one-time burner. The burner seals. You hold the note. A getaway pays a wallet you keep.
Crew API
The planned MCP tools and CLI for scripts and agents. Not live. Never a place to hand over a seed.

Keeping a note

If you do not have it, the desk cannot invent it.

Treat a note like a key you chose to hold. Back it up the way you back up other secrets you refuse to paste into a website. Heistly will not ask you to.

Where the contract goes

The $HEIST address, when it exists, will be printed on the token page and read from js/config.js. The field is empty until then. A message, a reply, or a look-alike site is not the printing.
