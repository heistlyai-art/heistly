File 01 — Personnel

Five seats. No extras.

A crew is not a crowd. Each role has a lane, a handoff, and something they are not allowed to do. Read the files before you give anyone a key to the desk.

Lookout Forger Banker Wheelman Tail

Seat 01 — Launches

Lookout

Does not buy

Watches
New token launches on Robinhood Chain and Solana.
Hands work to
Forger, with the name and which of your rules matched.
Will not
Place a trade, size a ticket, or talk a missed name back onto the sheet.

“New launch on Robinhood Chain. Demo name NIGHTGLASS. Six minutes old. It matches the sheet. File is yours.”

Sample line from the demo feed

Seat 02 — Contracts

Forger

Does not size

Watches
The contract, holder concentration, snipers, and honeypot risk.
Hands work to
The Banker, with a verdict: cleared, watch, or walk away.
Will not
Decide the amount. Sizing belongs to the Banker.

“Contract read on NIGHTGLASS. Dev share looks modest. Snipers are quiet. Honeypot check is clear. Verdict: cleared to size. I do not set the amount.”

Sample line from the demo feed

Seat 03 — The sheet

Banker

Can veto

Watches
Nightly spend, per-trade size, and whether a stop is actually on the ticket.
Hands work to
Wheelman, only after a clear. Or a stamp that says the job is over.
Will not
Raise the cap because a name is moving. The sheet does not negotiate at 2 a.m.

“Not tonight. A stop was not on the ticket, and I do not let a fresh launch leave without one. Veto. The cap stays intact.”

Sample line from the demo feed

Seat 04 — Execution

Wheelman

After a clear

Watches
The fill, the slippage, and the exits armed before the dust settles.
Hands work to
The position back to the desk, with the stop and the scale-out already set.
Will not
Enter a trade the Banker did not clear, or add size that was not on the ticket.

“Bought NIGHTGLASS. Exits are armed on the way in: scale out on the way up, hard stop under the entry. I am not adding.”

Sample line from the demo feed

Seat 05 — Wallets you name

Tail

Your list only

Watches
Only the wallets you labeled. No one you did not name.
Hands work to
Lookout and the morning file, with what those wallets did and when.
Will not
Invent a wallet, or follow one that is not already on your list.

“The wallet you labeled North Door moved. It bought the same demo name about a minute before our fill. No new wallet was added.”

Sample line from the demo feed
