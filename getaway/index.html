File 02 — Getaway

Pay any address. Leave your wallet off it.

A getaway is a private send. You name the address and the amount. The Driver, a relayer, posts the transaction and pays the gas. Your wallet does not sign that send, so the chain does not list you on it.

Order of a send

Same four beats. No shortcuts.

  1. 01

    Seal

    If the funds are not already a note, they enter the vault first. You cannot spend what you did not seal.

  2. 02

    Prove

    Your machine builds a proof that you hold an unspent note of the right asset and size. The proof does not say which note.

  3. 03

    Driver

    You hand the Driver a destination, not a seed. It pays the gas and submits. Your wallet stays out of the signature.

  4. 04

    Delivered

    The address is paid from the vault. You keep the note’s secret. There is no line from 0xA1…9C to 0x44…E2.

The Driver

A relayer with one job.

The Driver is not a second wallet of yours, and it is not a custodian. It exists so the gas for the outgoing send is paid by someone who is not you.

What the Driver sees

A proof, an amount, and a destination. Enough to post the transaction. Not your seed, and not a lasting map of every note you hold.

What the receiver sees

A payment from the vault. They do not need a Heistly account to receive it. They do not learn which deposit funded them.

Still a preview

The Driver is not on the road yet.

Nothing here will move funds. When the app opens, a getaway will follow this brief and not a quieter side door.
