File 02 — Method

Plan the job. Recruit the crew. Set the rules.

The desk does not improvise. You do three things before anyone is allowed to spend, and then the night belongs to the sheet.

Three steps

  1. 01

    Plan the job

    Decide what a night is for. Which chains are in play. Which hours the desk may work. What a launch must look like before Lookout is allowed to flag it. Write it once, while you are awake.

  2. 02

    Recruit the crew

    Seat the five roles. Lookout watches. Forger judges the contract. Banker guards the money. Wheelman executes. Tail follows wallets you named. If a seat is empty, that lane does not run.

  3. 03

    Set the rules

    Nightly spend cap. Per-trade size. Stop-loss. The wallet list for Tail. The Banker treats this as the law of the desk. A job that breaks it does not get a second hearing.

What goes on the sheet

The rules, in one place.

If it is not written here, the crew does not get to invent it at 3 a.m.

  • Chains in play: Robinhood Chain, Solana, or both.
  • Hours the desk is allowed to work.
  • What a launch must clear before Lookout flags it.
  • Nightly spend cap.
  • Per-trade size.
  • Stop-loss, required before Wheelman can leave.
  • Wallets Tail is allowed to follow, labeled by you.

A night on the desk

One handoff at a time.

This is the shape of a shift. The names in the Safehouse are a demo. The order of work is the product.

  1. 01:10

    Lookout

    Flags a launch that fits the sheet and passes the file to Forger.

  2. 01:12

    Forger

    Reads the contract and the holders. Returns a verdict. Does not name a size.

  3. 01:13

    Banker

    Sizes the ticket against the cap and the stop. Clears it, or stamps it aborted.

  4. 01:14

    Wheelman

    If cleared, buys or sells and sets the exits on the way in.

  5. 01:40

    Tail

    Notes whether a wallet you tagged moved. Adds nothing you did not ask for.

  6. Morning

    Debrief

    One dossier. Missions, wins, losses, the best score, and what the Banker blocked.

Custody

Bring your own wallet.

01

Your wallet

You connect a wallet you already control. Heistly does not issue you one and does not ask to hold it.

02

Signed on your side

Orders are signed through a secure wallet provider. The approval happens where your keys already live.

03

Nothing stored

Heistly does not take, hold, or store private keys or seed phrases. If a screen asks you to paste one here, it is not us.
