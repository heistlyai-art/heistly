File 05 — Questions

Before the job.

Straight answers. If the app is not open, this page says so. If a number is a demo, this page says so.

What is Heistly?

Heistly is a crew of five AI agents that work a market plan you wrote. They watch launches on Robinhood Chain and Solana, inspect contracts, guard a budget, place trades, and follow wallets you name. You read the night in a morning debrief.

Which markets can the crew watch?

Robinhood Chain and Solana. Lookout only flags launches that match rules you set. Anything off the sheet stays off the desk.

Is the Safehouse on this site live?

No. The feed here is a scripted demo with invented token names and made-up results. It shows how the crew hands work to each other. It is not a live market and not a record of real trades.

Does Heistly hold my keys?

No. You bring your own wallet. Keys and seed phrases stay in your custody, and orders are signed through a secure wallet provider. Heistly does not store them.

Can the crew trade past my limits?

No. The Banker exists to enforce the sheet: nightly spend, per-trade size, and stop-loss. If a job breaks the sheet, the Banker vetoes it. The stamp on that file reads aborted.

What does Tail follow?

Only wallets you choose and label yourself. Tail reports their moves into the Safehouse. Heistly does not hand you a list of strangers to copy.

What is $HEIST?

$HEIST is the crew's token, on Robinhood Chain. The contract address is not published yet. When it is, it will show on the token page. Heistly will not send you a contract in a direct message.

How does the Split work?

Fees from trades are divided by a published split. The initial cut is 40% buyback and liquidity, 30% crew rewards for active users, and 30% operations. That split may change. Every change is posted before it takes effect.

Is this financial advice?

No. Trading can go to zero. Demo names and numbers on this site are illustrative. Nothing here is a solicitation or a promise of profit.

When can I assemble a crew?

The app is not open yet. The button on this site stays on the brief until the desk opens.
