File 09 — FAQ

Ask these before you seal.

Straight answers. Where the product is still a brief, the answer says so. Nothing below is a live balance or a promise that every observer is blind.

Questions

What is Heistly?

Heistly is a private payments desk. You seal ETH, USDC, or USDG into an encrypted note. When you pay someone, a Driver posts the transaction and pays the gas. Your wallet is not on that send.

Which assets can I seal?

ETH, USDC, and USDG. A switch trades among those three inside the vault, so you do not withdraw to a public wallet just to change what you are holding.

Does my wallet pay the gas on a getaway?

No. The Driver is a relayer. It pays the gas and submits the proof. Your wallet does not sign the outgoing transaction, which is why it never touches that send.

Is the app available?

No. Heistly is in development. This website is the brief, not a wallet. There is no balance here and no way to send funds from these pages.

What does “no trail” mean?

The payout does not carry your address. The deposit into the vault is still public: the chain can see that assets went in. The note, which stays with you, is what connects that deposit to a later getaway. Heistly does not claim that every observer is blind.

What is a note?

A note is the encrypted record of what you sealed: the asset, the amount, and a secret only you can use. Heistly is not meant to keep a copy, and the desk never asks you to paste a seed phrase.

What is the Driver?

The Driver carries a getaway onto the chain. You build the proof where you sit. The Driver pays the gas and posts the send. It does not need your seed, and it does not choose who gets paid.

What is Safehouse recovery?

A one-time burner can seal funds in. It should not be the address that gets paid later. You hold the note, and a getaway pays a wallet you actually keep. The burner is not the receiver.

Which chains are escape routes?

Robinhood Chain, Base, Arbitrum, BNB Chain, and Solana. $HEIST itself is issued on Robinhood Chain. The contract is not published yet.

Where is the $HEIST contract?

It is not published. This site will print the address when it is real. Until then the field stays empty on purpose. Treat any contract sent to you in a message as a fake.

Do you hold my keys?

No. Notes and proofs are meant to stay on a machine you control. Heistly is not a custodian. If a screen asks you to paste a seed phrase, it is not this desk.

Can an agent pay through Heistly?

That is the Crew API: an MCP surface and a CLI for scripts and assistants. It is drawn in the brief, and it is not live. An agent can ask for a getaway. It does not receive your seed.
