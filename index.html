05 — Custody

Bring your own wallet.

Heistly is a desk, not a custodian. When the app opens, you connect a wallet you already control. Orders are signed on your side through a secure wallet provider.

Heistly does not take, hold, or store your private keys or your seed phrase. Custody stays where you left it.

A plain rule. If a screen ever asks you to paste a seed phrase into Heistly, it is not us.

07 — Before you sign

Questions worth asking first.

What is Heistly?

Heistly is a crew of five AI agents that work a market plan you wrote. They watch launches on Robinhood Chain and Solana, inspect contracts, guard a budget, place trades, and follow wallets you name. You read the night in a morning debrief.

Which markets can the crew watch?

Robinhood Chain and Solana. Lookout only flags launches that match rules you set. Anything off the sheet stays off the desk.

Is the Safehouse on this site live?

No. The feed here is a scripted demo with invented token names and made-up results. It shows how the crew hands work to each other. It is not a live market and not a record of real trades.

Does Heistly hold my keys?

No. You bring your own wallet. Keys and seed phrases stay in your custody, and orders are signed through a secure wallet provider. Heistly does not store them.
