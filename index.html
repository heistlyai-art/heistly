The order of work

Seal. Prove. Driver. Delivered.

Four beats, and they stay in that order. This is the plan we are building. The app is not open, and these cards do not run a payment.

  1. Seal

    ETH, USDC, or USDG enters the vault. You keep an encrypted note. The chain sees a deposit, not your next payment.

  2. Prove

    A zero-knowledge proof is built where you sit. It shows the note is yours and unspent, without naming which note.

  3. Driver

    The Driver, a relayer, pays the gas and posts the send. Your wallet does not sign it, so your address is not in it.

  4. Delivered

    The address you named is paid from the vault. You still hold the note. The public record shows a delivery, not your trail.

Before you seal

Questions worth asking first.

What is Heistly?

Heistly is a private payments desk. You seal ETH, USDC, or USDG into an encrypted note. When you pay someone, a Driver posts the transaction and pays the gas. Your wallet is not on that send.

Which assets can I seal?

ETH, USDC, and USDG. A switch trades among those three inside the vault, so you do not withdraw to a public wallet just to change what you are holding.

Does my wallet pay the gas on a getaway?

No. The Driver is a relayer. It pays the gas and submits the proof. Your wallet does not sign the outgoing transaction, which is why it never touches that send.

Is the app available?

No. Heistly is in development. This website is the brief, not a wallet. There is no balance here and no way to send funds from these pages.
