File 03 — The room

The Safehouse.

This is where the crew hands work across the desk. Lookout does not talk over Forger. Forger does not spend. The Banker can end a job in one line. What follows is a scripted night, labeled as a demo the whole way through.

Demo night log

Demo NIGHTGLASS, PAPERLANTERN, and BRASSKEY are invented. So are the figures.

Safehouse // night log

Demo feed

Scripted demo. Token names and figures are invented. Not a live feed.

Animation is off because reduced motion is on. The full demo transcript is below.

  1. 02:11:04 Flag

    Lookout → to Forger

    New launch on Robinhood Chain. Demo name NIGHTGLASS. Six minutes old. Liquidity sits above the floor you wrote. Age, chain, and size all match the sheet. Handing the file over.

  2. 02:12:19 Verdict

    Forger → to Banker

    Contract read on NIGHTGLASS. Dev share looks modest. Top holders are not stacked in one seat. Sniper pattern is quiet. Honeypot check comes back clear. Verdict: cleared to size. I do not set the amount.

  3. 02:12:41 Cleared

    Banker → to Wheelman

    Nightly cap still has room. This ticket fits the per-trade size. Stop is on the sheet. Cleared. If the fill slips past the stop distance, you abort.

  4. 02:13:02 Filled

    Wheelman → to Tail

    Bought NIGHTGLASS. Exits are armed on the way in: scale out on the way up, hard stop under the entry. I am not adding. The door is watched.

  5. 02:18:27 Shadow

    Tail → to Lookout

    The wallet you labeled North Door moved. It bought the same demo name about a minute before our fill. Noted in the log. No new wallet was added.

  6. 02:41:10 Flag

    Lookout → to Forger

    Second print, this one on Solana. Demo name PAPERLANTERN. It matches the sheet on age and liquidity. Sending it through. Still a demo name, not a recommendation.

  7. 02:42:02 Walk

    Forger → to Banker

    PAPERLANTERN fails the holder test. One seat holds far too much. Sniper wallets arrived in the first seconds. Verdict: walk away. I will not dress this up.

  8. 02:42:11 Veto

    Banker → to Wheelman

    Agreed, and I would have stopped it anyway. The size on the ticket would have broken the nightly cap. Job aborted. Stamp the file.

  9. 03:05:44 Flag

    Lookout → to Forger

    Robinhood Chain again. Demo name BRASSKEY. Rules match. Liquidity is real enough for the sheet. Your read.

  10. 03:06:30 Verdict

    Forger → to Banker

    BRASSKEY is clean enough to size. Dev share is small. Top holders are spread out. Honeypot check is clear. Verdict: cleared. The call on risk sits with the Banker.

  11. 03:06:48 Veto

    Banker → to Wheelman

    Not tonight. A stop was not on the ticket, and I do not let a fresh launch leave without one. Veto. The cap stays intact.

  12. 03:22:15 Exit

    Wheelman → to Banker

    NIGHTGLASS hit the scale-out. Sold the planned slice. The rest rides on the hard stop. Bankroll update is in the margin. Demo figures only.

  13. 06:00:00 Close

    Banker → to Crew

    Night is closed. Two jobs aborted by this desk. One demo name scaled out. The debrief is on the table. No one spends until the sheet is read.

How to read a line

Flags on the log.

  1. Flag

    Lookout

    A launch matched your rules. The file moves to Forger. Nothing has been bought.

  2. Verdict

    Forger

    Cleared, watch, or walk away, after the contract and the holders. Still no size.

  3. Veto

    Banker

    The job breaks the sheet. Wheelman stands down. The morning stamp says aborted.

Cleared and Filled mean the Banker allowed it and Wheelman moved. Shadow is Tail, reporting a wallet you already labeled. Close is the Banker shutting the night.
